Fully separate from the strategy above — its own paper balance, its own tables, not part of the KPIs or P&L at the top of this page. "Realistic" fills wait a simulated pipeline delay before pricing, the same way real execution would lag the wallet; "if filled at wallet's price" shows what the same trade would have made with zero lag, isolating how much of the result is pure latency cost.
